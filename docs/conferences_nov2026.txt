(sections 1-4). One paper goes to one venue at a time: the same paper at two of
these is dual submission, and ICIDA does not allow a submitted paper to be
withdrawn. Two clearly different papers (own contribution, own results, no
shared text) can go to two venues.

Bottom line (20 Oct)
--------------------
No reputable conference (IEEE / Springer / Elsevier) can both ACCEPT and
PUBLISH a paper submitted now before 20 Nov. Proceedings go online 2-6 months
after the conference. What is possible: an ACCEPTANCE LETTER before 20 Nov.

Patent: the project is also a patent disclosure. File the patent application
BEFORE any paper or preprint is made public, or the publication can count as
prior art against it.
